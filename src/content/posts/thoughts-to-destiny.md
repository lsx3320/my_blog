> 这段教诫以英文流传甚广，常被归为佛教/佛法的教言，此处原文照录，并附中译。

> Watch your thoughts, because they will become your words.
>
> Watch your words, because they will become your actions.
>
> Watch your actions, because they will become your behavior.
>
> Watch your behavior, because it will become your habits.
>
> Watch your habits, because they will become your character.
>
> Watch your character, because it will become your destiny.

**中译：**

> 观照你的念头，因为它会变成你的言语。
>
> 观照你的言语，因为它会变成你的行为。
>
> 观照你的行为，因为它会变成你的习惯。
>
> 观照你的习惯，因为它会变成你的性格。
>
> 观照你的性格，因为它会成为你的命运。

丙午年季秋之初九
